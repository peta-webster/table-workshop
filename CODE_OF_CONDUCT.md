# Community expectations

Be respectful, discuss the work, and make room for different levels of experience. Constructive criticism and reproducible bug reports are welcome. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict participation when necessary. For conduct concerns, use GitHub's reporting tools or contact the maintainer through a contact method listed on their public profile. Do not post private incident details in a public issue.

## 中文

请尊重其他参与者，围绕问题和代码讨论，理解不同经验水平。欢迎建设性意见和可复现的问题报告。请勿骚扰、歧视、人身攻击、威胁或公开他人隐私。

维护者可以编辑或移除干扰协作的内容，必要时限制参与。行为问题可使用 GitHub 举报功能，或通过维护者公开个人主页列出的联系方式反映；不要在公开 Issue 中披露私人事件细节。
